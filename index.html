Orbit Browser

Floating bubble web browser for Android

Orbit keeps a web page in a small, draggable bubble that floats above your other apps. Tap the bubble to open the page, drag it wherever it is out of the way, and swipe up on the bottom toolbar to put it back. No app switching, no losing your place.

What it does

  • A real browser in a bubble. Open any site in a resizable floating window while you keep using another app behind it.
  • Minimise and restore. Collapse the window back into the bubble and pick up exactly where you left off.
  • Reading without the clutter. A reading mode strips ads and navigation when you want the text only.
  • Save links to your own accounts. Send the page you are reading to a bookmark or read-later service you have connected.

Integrations

Raindrop.io — connect your own Raindrop.io account and save the current page in one tap. Orbit stores an access token on your device; the token is excluded from cloud backup and from device transfer. You can disconnect at any time in Settings.

Instapaperplanned
Support is in development and is not part of the current release.

Orbit only ever sends a page to these services when you tap the save button. It never sends anything on its own, and it does not receive a copy of what you save.
